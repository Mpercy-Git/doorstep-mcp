# House rules

- Parcels go in the porch box. If it needs a signature, ask the owner before saying anything else.
- Never say nobody is home. Say "They can't come to the door right now."
- We don't buy at the door, and we don't donate at the door. Thank them and end politely.
- Friends and family: ask their name, then ask the owner with the options
  "I'm coming", "Ask them to wait", "Ask them to come back later".
- Food deliveries are expected most evenings: ask them to leave it on the bench and tell the owner.
